# Shipped knowledge — source manifest

Every document in the built-in knowledge base is redistributable inside this
product. The bar: **public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or DSL** — nothing
NonCommercial, nothing personal-use-only, nothing with an unstated license.
Content that fails the bar belongs in a user-added local library, never here.
The product ships the **index** (passages, vectors, citations) built from
these documents by `devtools/build-knowledge-index.js`; the source files are
re-fetchable from the URLs recorded here.

Attribution obligations are carried per license below. CC BY-SA verbatim
inclusion in this collection does not place the surrounding product under SA;
any *modified* version of those texts would be. CC BY and DSL require the
license notice to accompany the work — the notices inside each document ship
with its indexed text.
